Same complaint, different agency

10M+ reports a year · 12 agencies · 1,292:1 imbalance, on South Korea's nationwide civic reporting platform. The right agency depends on where a report was filed, not just what it says. Deployed fix: geocode to 1 of 229 districts, prepend the district as two plain tokens.

    How a coordinate becomes two tokens

    One build step + one string operation. No vocab additions, no architecture change, no extra serving path. Released code = the exact production logic.
